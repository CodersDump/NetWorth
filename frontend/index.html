Feature settings
Let new players create a profile instantly (skip approval)

Off by default: a new sign-up's "create profile" goes to the request queue above. Turn on during a busy session so new people can start playing without waiting. Claiming an existing profile always needs approval.

Show levels, coins, store & quests to everyone

Off during soft launch (admin-only preview). Flip on to reveal the whole gamification layer - level under the banner, coin balance, the Store tab, and weekly quests - to all players.

Maintenance

Replays every match to rebuild ratings, XP, levels and coins from scratch. Use after a manual data fix, or to backfill XP onto players from before the XP system.

Match reorder

Matches count in timestamp order, which sets every Elo change. Pick a day this week, drag rows into the real order, and apply - timestamps swap and ratings recompute. Past weeks are settled and locked.
